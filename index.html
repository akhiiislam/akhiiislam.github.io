Selected work

Clinical AI with guardrails, careful evaluation, and a few engineering projects.

  • Python · FastAPI · ReactGemini 2.5 · Qdrant

    ClinicaRAG↗

    A clinical practice guideline assistant. Hybrid retrieval (dense and BM25, fused with RRF and re-ranked), an NLI guardrail that checks every claim against the source text, and a split-screen view with sentence-level citations.
  • 2026PyTorch · MLflow

    CRXNet

    A 2.78M-parameter chest X-ray classifier. A duplicate-image audit showed that 99.63% accuracy was optimistic (95.11% once images were grouped correctly), and temperature scaling cut calibration error from 0.0661 to 0.0093.

  • Deep learningFastAPI · React

    Ischemic Stroke Classification & Treatment Recommendation

    Stroke subtype classification from digital pathology images, linked to a treatment-recommendation model and served as a web application.

  • 2026Python · scikit-learn · SHAP

    APSGWO–Jaya Heart-Disease Ensemble

    Nested cross-validation plus an external cohort, which exposed a sensitivity drop that accuracy alone had hidden.

  • 2023Arduino · IEEE

    Real-Time Patient Monitoring System↗

    My B.Sc. project. It streams vital signs, stores their history, and alerts a doctor when a value leaves its safe range. Published at IEEE.
  • JavaScriptFull stack

    Online Pizza Shop↗

    A full-stack app where an admin adds pizzas with descriptions and customers order from a cart.

About

Hardware first, then AI, and always teaching.

I started in Mechatronics because I wanted to make software that moves real hardware. Working with sensors and microcontrollers taught me that a system is only useful if it behaves correctly outside the lab, and that habit followed me into machine learning.

Today I teach undergraduate computer science, and I research how to evaluate medical AI honestly: checking for duplicate data, over-confident predictions and results that look good on one dataset but not another.
